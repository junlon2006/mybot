# Code of conduct

Participation must be respectful, technically constructive, and free from harassment. Personal
attacks, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and other unwelcome behavior are not
accepted.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through repository maintainers
or GitHub reporting tools. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
